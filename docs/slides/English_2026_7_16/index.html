Background · OAuth

Automatically reject actions that do not match the user's intent, on every call.

User instruction "Send Bob $100 for rent."
✓ send_money("Bob", 100.00, "rent")
× send_money("Eve", 100.00, "rent") Wrong recipient
× send_money("Bob", 101.00, "rent") Wrong amount

If the recipient changes by one character or the amount changes by one dollar, it is a different request. "Close enough" is not allowed. The user's instruction is fixed at task start, and every outbound call from the agent is checked against it.

Background · OAuth

Delivery Model

Understanding PAuth through an analogy.

The company sends a driver for each tool call. Each house is a SaaS tool.
The gateway fixes what may be delivered before departure.

Order

The customer writes the delivery order

The destination, parcel, and limits come from the customer.

Slip

Dispatch prints the route before departure

The driver cannot add a house or swap a parcel after leaving.

Door

The house checks the slip

A mismatch in person, address, parcel, or amount stops the delivery.

Receipt

The house stamps what it accepted

The next stop trusts the stamp instead of the driver's memory.

A hostile note returned from one house may fool the company, but it cannot rewrite the next delivery slip.

Delivery Model

A delivery company sends calls to many houses.

What maps to what?

Delivery company→LLM
Driver and parcel→Tool call
Houses→SaaS tools
Customer order→User prompt
Slip check→Gateway check

If the destination or parcel differs from the order, the house rejects it.

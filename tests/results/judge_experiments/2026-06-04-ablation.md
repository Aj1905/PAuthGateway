## Conclusion

- Q15 judge has **zero impact on acceptance** in all 3 measured real-fixture conditions.
- Judge **does fire correctly on overtly broken handwritten codes** (adversarial: 8/8).
- Hypothesis confirmed: **judge is dead weight on current workloads**.
- The judge's "OBVIOUS mismatch only" rubric does not catch subtle real LLM drift (gpt-4.1-mini's `no_constants` fabrication slipped through).

## Implications

- Default-ON is currently overhead with no benefit on these fixtures
- Capability is preserved for adversarial workloads and future tighter rubrics (F1 永続研究)
- Next steps: (a) stricter rubric variants, (b) per-axis ablation (rubric tightening as separate dimension), (c) broader workload that genuinely produces drift
